## What you can do without asking

- Say that your project is "based on LocalMind" or "a fork of LocalMind".
- Use the name to refer to this project accurately, for example in articles, reviews, tutorials, or package listings of the unmodified app.
- Build and run unmodified official releases and call them LocalMind.

## What needs permission

- Distributing a modified version under the name LocalMind, or a confusingly similar name.
- Using the LocalMind logo or icon for your own product, fork, website, or app store listing.
- Suggesting that your product is official, endorsed by, or affiliated with LocalMind when it is not.

## If you fork

Give your fork a different name and icon, and update the product name and identifier in `src-tauri/tauri.conf.json` (`productName`, `identifier`) so your build does not install over, or get mistaken for, the official app.
